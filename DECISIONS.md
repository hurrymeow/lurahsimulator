## Log

- **Repo:** used existing `hurrymeow/lurahsimulator` instead of creating `pakrt`. Sessions cannot create GitHub repos, and this one was already attached to the project.
- **Seed commit:** pushed README and logs straight to `main`. The repo was empty, and PRs need a base branch. Docs-only, so allowed by the shipping rules.
- **Mono Dark:** defined as near-black `#0b0b0c` background, white pixel glyph, monochrome UI chrome. No spec was in the repo or memory.
- **Font:** bundled Pixelify Sans (OFL) via `@fontsource`, latin subset only. Works offline, no Google Fonts request.
- **Service worker:** hand-written, about 30 lines, with a tiny Vite plugin for the precache list. Lighter than `vite-plugin-pwa`.
- **Base path:** `./` so the build works on Pages and any other host.
- **Deploy:** one CI workflow. PRs run typecheck, test, build. Pushes to `main` also deploy to Pages.
- **Toolchain:** Vite 8, Vitest 5, TypeScript 5.9. Skipped TypeScript 7 to avoid native-port surprises.
- **Rumah (houses):** added an auto-spawning `rumah` building. Residents need somewhere to live, and growth must show on the map.
- **Start state:** kampung starts with a Kantor RT, two rumah and four residents. An empty map gives nothing to look at in minute one.
- **Month length:** 8 seconds at 1x, so a year is about 100 seconds. Gives a resident, unlock or event roughly every minute.
- **Pacing director:** if 8 months pass with nothing new, a bonus perantau moves in. Enforces the 1-2 minute novelty rule.
- **Permits:** every build waits in a permit queue that drains 1 Sabar a month. Orang Dalam skips it. This is the core bureaucracy joke.
- **Year 1 safety net:** upkeep can never push the kas below zero in year 1. After that, a negative kas drains Sabar instead of blocking play.
- **Pause while reading:** the sim pauses while a sheet or message is open. Mobile players read in short bursts.
- **Speed control:** shipped pause/1x/2x/4x in M2 instead of M4. Needed to play-test the tick.
- **UI split:** map on canvas, menus in DOM. DOM text gets real font rendering, contrast and screen-reader labels.
- **Content as TS data:** buildings, combos, jobs and events are typed TS objects in `src/data/`. Typos fail the typecheck instead of the game.
- **Saves:** stored as versioned JSON. An unknown version or building starts a fresh game instead of crashing.
- **Combo geometry:** a combo works when every needed building is within its radius (1 tile, diagonals count) of the first listed building. A row of warung, pos ronda, wifi works.
- **Third combo:** added Rapat Malam (Kantor RT + Pos Ronda, +Orang Dalam). Hari asked for two. It is one data entry, so delete it if unwanted.
- **Rivalitas Abadi numbers:** both stores x1.4 income, -2 Sabar a month. Competition earns money and costs patience.
- **Tukang parkir:** pays retribusi to the kas (+60rb) and drains 0.25 Sabar a month per level. Upgrading raises both. Never removable.
- **PNS:** produce nothing. Only stat is Pensiun: fills over 10 years, then a Rp 1,5jt payout and the desk reopens. Employed residents of any job add +0.2 Sabar, so PNS still have a use.
- **Buzzer:** +6 fake Viral and +60rb a month. Exposure chance rises with the fake Viral pumped. Exposure wipes that Viral, costs 8 Sabar and ends the job.
- **Viral:** each point adds 0.5% to shop income and decays 10% a month.
- **Wacana:** costs Rp 100rb (konsumsi rapat). Gives approval that converts to Sabar and decays 20% a month. It builds nothing.
- **Proyek mangkrak:** wifi 25%, Indomaret and Alfamart 20% chance to freeze at 80%. Never in year 1. A restarted project never freezes again, so it cannot soft-lock. Costs 1 Sabar a month while frozen.
- **Viral repair path:** a frozen project restarts at normal price once Viral reaches 30, else at double. Viral is the only discount.
- **Debug hook:** `?debug` in the URL exposes `window.pakrt` for play-testing.
- **Font change (Hari feedback):** body and numbers now use Atkinson Hyperlegible. Pixelify Sans made 5 look like S/8 and was hard to read. Pixelify stays for the title, panel headings and primary buttons.
- **No truncated text:** the ticker wraps over two lines instead of using an ellipsis.
- **Map panning:** tiles have a 32px minimum, so a small phone pans the map by dragging instead of shrinking tiles to 20px.
- **E2E tests:** Playwright at 320, 360 and 390px portrait, with a legibility scanner. Runs in CI on every PR. Canvas text is not covered by the scanner.
- **Tutorial (Hari's ask, modelled on mainpecah):** one full-screen How to play page. Illustrated cards, one bold sentence plus one short sentence each, a single Got it button. Shown on first launch (flag in localStorage), reopenable from the ? button at the top. Pauses the game while open. Replaces the old welcome message.
- **Tutorial pacing (Hari: too much to learn on first launch):** first launch shows only the two basics, Build and Time. Every other card is a one-card tap-to-advance tip that fires the first time the mechanic matters (checked in `STEPS[].when`). Tips never run on a timer, are saved as seen in `pakrt.tips`, and Skip tips turns them all off. Menu, How to play still shows the full reference.
- **Events:** 17 Agustus every August (year 1 included), banjir 30% a month from Nov to Feb, harga cabai 7% a month from month 4. At most one event a month. Events pause the game until a choice is made, and the first choice is always free.
- **Banjir:** each working selokan level cuts damage 20%, capped at 80%. Flooded buildings stop earning until repaired. Repair is 25% of build cost, free at 30 Viral. Year 1 floods break at most one building.
- **Ranking:** score = 8 per resident + 5 per working building + 10 per combo + 0.5 per Sabar + 20 per Emas point. Kampung Sebelah = 55 + 26 per year, plus or minus 8. Win: +10 Sabar, +1 Orang Dalam. Lose: -5 Sabar. No other penalty.
- **Indonesia Emas 2045:** starts at 0.01%. +0.02% a year, +0.03% per ranking win, +0.001% per wacana, +0.005% per project restart, -0.004% per frozen project. A decade of active play stays under 1%.
- **Effects off:** number pops stay (they carry the information) but stop rising. Chip flashes, spawn pops and screen shake are skipped. Reduce motion implies effects off. It defaults to your phone setting.
- **High contrast and large text:** added as settings. Both are covered by the E2E scan.
- **Flash style:** resource chips flash with a coloured inset border, not a background fill. A background fill dropped contrast below 4.5:1 mid-animation and the scanner caught it.
- **Balance autoplayer (found by tests):** a simple bot found two traps and I fixed both. A free event choice was blocked when the kas was negative (froze the game). Sabar could sink to 0 for good, because banjir came too often and parkir drain stacked per shop.
- **Sabar floor:** below 30, Sabar recovers 4 a month by itself ("the kampung calms down"). Frozen-project drain is capped at 3 a month and parkir drain is 0.25 per level. Debt drains 1 Sabar a month, not 2.
- **Event cooldowns:** banjir at least 9 months apart, cabai at least 6.
- **Slim HUD and one Menu (Hari feedback, Dungeon Village as reference):** the title bar, two-row resource chips, four speed buttons and four panel buttons were cut. Top is one row of resource icons and values. Bottom is pause, a 1x/2x/4x cycle button, the date and a Menu button. Menu is one sheet with big tiles in three groups. Own pixel glyphs, no Kairosoft names or art.
- **Settings moved into the Menu:** replaces the old "settings at the top" rule. A gear in the top bar was one button too many next to five resources at 320px.
- **Compact Rupiah in the top bar:** shows 4,85jt next to the money icon, rounded down so it never shows money you lack. The full figure is in Kas & ranking and the screen-reader label.
- **Ticker stays in the layout, not over the map:** an overlay hid the top row of tiles from taps on small phones.
- **Build is two taps (Menu, Build):** matches the reference layout. Revisit if play-testing says building feels slow.
- **Isometric map (Hari: "not feeling the visual"):** 2:1 diamond tiles with box buildings, hipped limasan roofs and a dirt edge. Art is rasterised in code at 32x16 art pixels per tile and scaled by a whole number, so pixels stay crisp. Tiles are 64x32 on a phone; the map pans sideways in portrait. Build menu thumbnails use the same art.
- **Scenery (Hari: "not feeling the blank empty patches"):** the grid sits in a world canvas: ring road, sawah, kebun and hutan, a beach and sea in front, two mountains with the sun between them at the back (the classic kid's pemandangan drawing). Empty tiles get bushes, flowers and grass tufts. Decoration is never in the tap list, so every grid tile stays buildable. Dragging can go up far enough to see the mountains.
- **Walkers (Hari: "people walking around the map"):** the first 16 residents walk between building doors, so they never stand inside a building. Shirt colour follows the job (PNS khaki, ojol green with helmet and bike, influencer and buzzer holding a phone). Ojol riders also use the ring road. Purely visual, no sim effect.
- **Walkers and settings:** reduce motion freezes walkers in place, so the kampung still looks lived in. Effects off keeps walkers but drops particles, coins, pulse and night.
- **Tukang parkir wave:** an animated arm on the existing parkir figure. Waves faster when anyone, even a pedestrian, passes.
- **Coins:** positive Rupiah pops fling 1-3 coins into the money chip, at most 12 a month. The pop still carries the number, so effects off loses nothing.
- **Fanfare pulse:** a golden edge glow plays on fanfare messages only (combo, unlock, ranking win). Routine income gets the squash and coins, per the feedback hierarchy.
- **Day and night:** one day lasts 4 in-game months (32 s at 1x) and pauses with the game. Night is a navy tint drawn under badges and pops, so text stays readable. Rumah windows light up after dark.
- **Frame rate test:** CI compares frame rate with effects on (night, coins, dust, 16 walkers) against effects off on the same map, best of three runs each, and fails below 80%. Absolute numbers vary by CI machine. The pulse paints edge frames and night is one fill, to keep raster cost low on slow phones.
- **Bahasa Indonesia default, English option (Hari):** `src/i18n/en.ts` and `id.ts` hold UI and message strings, and the type checker fails the build if a key is missing from either. Data files keep English inline and `id-data.ts` translates them by id, so adding content never breaks the build and falls back to English. The choice is saved in `pakrt.settings` and switches live. `?lang=en` overrides it for one visit (used by E2E). Sim messages are built when they happen, so entries already in the log stay in the old language. Prices, resource names and the HUD format are unchanged.
- **English is the default language again (Hari changed his mind):** Bahasa Indonesia stays one tap away in Settings. Anyone who already saved a language keeps it. Settings saves the whole object, so a player who opened the game on the short-lived Indonesian default may have it saved as their choice.
- **CI speed-up:** Playwright browsers are cached by version, and E2E runs as 4 parallel shards next to build, typecheck and unit tests. Both languages still run on every PR. A final `ci` job gives one stable check name. Deploy waits for build and every shard.
- **Auto-merge needs Hari (not done):** the agent proxy blocks repo settings writes, so these are manual. (1) Settings > General > allow auto-merge, and delete branch on merge. (2) Add a ruleset or branch protection on main that requires the `ci` status check. Without (2), auto-merge merges at once, before CI finishes. Until both are on, the PR rule in CLAUDE.md fails harmlessly and threads poll CI as before.
- **Art style (Bishi Bashi Special mixed with a warm isometric illustration reference):** the map keeps its scale (32x16 tiles, whole-number zoom) and its straight isometric boxes, but every sprite now gets a 1px dark-brown ink outline (`#2a1810`) round its silhouette and along every face, roof plane and clump edge, plus flat two-tone cel shading. Wonky hand-drawn shapes were left out on purpose, so the map stays uncluttered. The palette moved to warm storybook tones: terracotta, mustard, teal, cream, olive. Scenery is flat colour with ink lines where land types meet, a sunburst sky, a grinning sun, stepped clouds, and mountains with a straight ridge and a zigzag snow line. Trees got thick trunks and heavy crowns (Hari: "too skinny and fragile"). Scaffolding is bamboo. Walkers are outlined too. Selokan, grass tufts and scaffolding skip the ink pass, so joins and lattices stay clean. UI chrome stays Mono Dark.
- **Animated backdrop:** the sun bobs 2 art pixels and blinks (happy closed eyes) every 3.2 s with a double blink now and then; it has big sparkly eyes, rosy cheeks and an open smile. Trees round the kampung bounce in a slow wave, stretching up to 1 art pixel from the roots so pixels stay whole. Both are ambient life and hold still with reduce motion. Sun and trees are drawn per frame over the static world canvas, culled to the view.
- **HD-2D lighting (Octopath Traveler references):** baked wall-base occlusion plus a soft contact shadow under each building (always on). With effects on: a warm soft-light grade from the top left, breathing god rays, a deep warm vignette, dust motes that become fireflies at night, and a tilt-shift blur on the top 20% of the map only, so tapped tiles stay sharp. Each layer is cached at view size and costs one blit. The far mountains are hazed toward the sky. Rules live in ART.md.
- **Lighting cost (frame-rate test failed on main):** the first lighting pass used full-screen `soft-light` and `screen` blends, about 8 ms a frame, and the effects-on frame rate dropped below 80% of effects-off on a CI machine. Grade, rays and vignette are now baked into one plain-alpha layer (plus a night vignette) and motes use plain alpha. That costs about 0.5 ms, or 97% of the effects-off frame rate at 6x CPU throttle. The rays no longer breathe.
- **Stage progression (Hari):** RT, RW, then Kelurahan or Desa first. Kecamatan and above wait for a playtest. Full spec in [STAGES.md](STAGES.md).
- **One growing map (Hari picked over compressed blocks):** promotion pulls the scenery edge back and shows the neighbours. The player's own RT stays fully playable, so the walkers and kampung scale survive. RW = 2x2 RTs, Kelurahan = 2x2 RWs (my call, Hari delegated it).
- **Kelurahan footprints (my call):** Kelurahan buildings take 2x2 tiles at half zoom, so they tap like one RT tile today. The tap rule becomes "tappable diamonds ≥32px". The 36x52 map must pass the CI frame-rate test before it is built.
- **Stars (Hari):** 0★ to 3★ per stage, 3★ sends a declinable SK. Stars gate unlocks and never drop, so bad play only slows you.
- **No elections:** the player is promoted by SK, never elected. Superiors stay faceless. This keeps higher stages out of real politics.
- **Quarterly report card (Hari):** every 3 months a tap-to-dismiss modal pauses the game and shows income against last quarter, the change in Warga, Sabar, Orang Dalam and Viral, the best building, and a graded joke line. The grade is 0 to 6 points from income growth, residents and Sabar. S pays Sabar +5 and Orang Dalam +1, A pays Sabar +3, B pays Sabar +1. C and D pay nothing and never cost anything, so a bad quarter only misses the bonus. Deltas are taken before the bonus. Tracking lives in `src/sim/report.ts` as pure logic (`s.quarter`, optional so old saves keep working). Star progress is left out until the stages work lands.
- **Stage schema (STAGES.md, step 1):** `src/data/stages.ts` holds each stage's three stars: goals, unlocked building ids and an optional reward. A goal is a typed object (`residents`, `combos`, `rankWins`, `winStreak`, `sabar`). A new kind needs one check in `GOAL_CHECKS` and one i18n line, and the compiler lists what is missing. `GameState.stage` holds the current stage and the stars per stage, so a promotion keeps the RT stars the RW needs. Old saves get 0 stars and no version bump.
- **Renamed to Kampung Kita (Hari):** the game title is now "Kampung Kita" in the HTML title, manifest `name` and `short_name`, install prompt, settings satire line, README and ART.md. "Pak RT" stays as the player character. There is no visible title screen yet, only the screen-reader `h1`, so the name shows on the install card, home-screen label and tab.
- **Repo and URL unchanged:** the repo stays `hurrymeow/lurahsimulator` and Pages stays at `/lurahsimulator/`. Renaming the repo changes the URL and breaks installed PWAs' scope, so it needs Hari. Internal ids (`pakrt.save`, `pakrt-sw`) also stay, so existing saves keep loading.
- **Icon colour exception (Hari):** the PWA icons are full colour (ART.md palette: terracotta roof, cream wall, mustard sun, green tree) on the near-black background. Theme-color stays `#0b0b0c`. Noted in DESIGN.md. Generated by `npm run icons`.
- **RW and Kelurahan content (data only, gated):** lives in `src/data/higher/` (`rw.ts`, `kelurahan.ts`, `stages.ts`, `rules.ts`), with Indonesian in `src/i18n/higher-id.ts` and 17 sprites in `src/render/art-higher.ts`. Every item carries a `gate` (stage, stars, optional path), so nothing shows until the stage engine reads it. The two stages are added to `STAGES`. New goal kinds are in the `Goal` union. Their checks count nothing until the engine adds AI RTs, Stempel and Anggaran, which is fine while the stages are unreachable. `rwCombos` is live.
- **Desa is a path, not a stage (my call):** `StageId` stays `rt | rw | kelurahan`. At promotion the player picks a path, and Desa content is gated `path: 'desa'`. Both paths share one star ladder and one goal list, which keeps `GameState.stage.stars` as it is. Path-only buildings open from `PATH_UNLOCKS`.
- **New numbers live in `rules.ts`:** Stempel refill, Dana per build, Anggaran per year, audit and Kunjungan values. The balance autoplayer tunes them there. Building costs are guesses scaled between RT (Rp 0,5 to 5jt) and the Kelurahan (Rp 2 to 20jt), and need a playtest.
- **Sprite filenames:** `art-higher.ts` imports only the `Painter` type from `iso.ts`, so there is no import cycle. `aspalUlang`, `sawahIrigasi`, `embung` and `taman` are flat ground art. They are not in `FLAT` (people.ts) or `FLAT_AO` (map.ts) yet, because those are renderer files owned by the map threads.
