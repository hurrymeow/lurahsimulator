# Growing map engine

- Map grows with the stage: 9x13, 18x26, 36x52. Promotion moves nothing the player built.
- AI RTs build when funded, earn stars by the RT checklist, pay iuran into the Kas RW and count in the area ranking.
- RW Sebelah and Kelurahan Sebelah replace Kampung Sebelah as the rival.
- Neighbours panel in the Menu funds each RT. Tapping an AI RT opens it.
- Stempel meter, and the RW goal checks: funded RTs, RTs and RWs at a star, wins by tier, months without debt.
- Gated RW and Kelurahan buildings are in the registry, behind their stage and star gate.
- Frame rate: only visible tiles cost anything. A packed Kelurahan runs at about 70% of a packed RT.
- Not done: RW content effects (combos, jobs, events), Anggaran, 2x2 footprints, Kelurahan zoom, Desa path.
