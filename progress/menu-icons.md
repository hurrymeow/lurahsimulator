# Menu icons

Redrew every Menu tile glyph as 12x12 pixel art (white and grey, mustard on the key part): house for Build, two people for Residents, megaphone for Policies, book for Combo Book, notepad for Log, bar chart for Treasury, question mark for How to play, gear for Settings, tray arrow for Install. Residents and Neighbours no longer fall back to the shared people icon. The unused `menu` glyph is gone.
