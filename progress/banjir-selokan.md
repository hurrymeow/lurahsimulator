# The banjir is back, so the Selokan unlocks

The Selokan was not deleted. It opens on the first banjir, and since ticks became weeks the banjir had become about twelve times rarer, so most games never met one.
- Random seasonal events can now come in any week of their months, at most once a season.
- The first banjir is sure in the first rainy season, from week 40.
- Saves that never had one get it in the next rainy season.
- The scorecard bots keep two Selokan after the first flood.
