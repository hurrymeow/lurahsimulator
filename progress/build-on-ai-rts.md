# Build on AI RTs

The player can build RT buildings on an AI-run RT's free lots. They count for that RT, and the AI builds round them. Kunjungan (1 Orang Dalam, once a quarter per RT) makes the RT build now and adds 3 warga. Go to RT pans the map to it. Takeover and hand-back are gone from the sheet. See `decisions/build-on-ai-rts.md`.
