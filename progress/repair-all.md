# Repair all

A small chip under the satellite view shows "Repair all (n) · cost" only while buildings or paths are damaged. One tap repairs them cheapest first, as far as the Rupiah goes. Mangkrak buildings are not included, they still restart from the inspector. Narrow phones (under 360px) put the chip bottom-right.

Follows the docs: slim HUD, no new button row, EN and ID strings.
