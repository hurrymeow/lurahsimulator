# House silhouettes

Rumah can be upgraded to level 3: a teras at level 2 (5 beds) and a rumah tingkat at level 3 (7 beds), in every roof shape and colour. The kos-kosan is a tall two-floor kost with an outside stair from level 1, and grows a third floor with a flat roof, tandon and jemuran at level 2. Kontrakan is unchanged. Tests: beds per level, demolish uses the level's beds, roof shapes at every rumah level, plus the existing level-progression art checks.
