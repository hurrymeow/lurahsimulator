# Playtest: RT 3★ to Kelurahan 1★

An agent playtest of the range T7 handed over, as the nine scorecard player types plus a screen playthrough. Seven fixes:
- A saved event card no longer covers the home screen.
- At 3★ the goal line says to accept the SK in Menu.
- Kampung Sebelah joins with starter lanes, and Inem's notes say to build beside a path.
- A reopened game pans to Kampung Sebelah during its build step.
- A building can no longer cut another building's only path.
- An open RW combo goal names the cheapest RW combo and what it needs first.
- Grup Chat Warga is hidden from the RW goal while it cannot happen.

Star times for the player types are unchanged, except that the random placer gets further and the hint reader is a little slower. Screenshots are in `progress/playtest-rt3-kel1/`. Details are in `decisions/playtest-rt3-kel1.md`.
