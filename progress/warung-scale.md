# Warung Sembako scale

2026-11-08. Playtest: the level 1 warung tenda looked far bigger than the other level 1 buildings.
Measured every 1x1 sprite: the tenda stood 22px tall and 25px wide, against 13 to 16px tall and
17 to 21px wide for the rumah, pos ronda, rumah makan and barbershop at level 1. Levels 2 and 3 were
also above their peers.

- Level 1 tenda: smaller terpal, shorter poles and counter. 16px tall, 18px wide (was 22 x 25).
- Level 2 gerobak: smaller lean-to and cart. 17px tall, 21px wide (was 20 x 26), like a level 2 shop.
- Level 3 toko kelontong: one 11px storey instead of 13. 20px tall (was 22), like the barbershop and
  rumah makan at level 3. The retired Toko Kelontong, which draws the same art, shrinks with it.

Same looks, materials and props (ART.md, Building levels); only the scale changed.
