# One isometric world

2026-11-02. The kampung's island now sits in an open sea with no sky. Twin volcanoes rise from its back corner in the same lit facets as the hills, the coast wanders in bays and headlands with a beach all round, and 15 to 18 iso islands of mixed sizes dot the sea. The painted mountains and the sun are gone. See `decisions/iso-backdrop.md`. The land rises gradually from every shore to the volcanoes, and most of the green land is wooded with scenery trees at 70% size. Karst knolls ring the kampung with trees at their feet, and scenery trees vary in size (palms excepted). Trees are small native sprites with a layered canopy, oil-palm plantations sit round the volcanoes, and PLN poles with messy cables ring the road.
