# The SK as a ceremony, and combos that wait on an upgrade

- The SK card names the new post ("Your SK: Pak RW!") and has its own accept button. While it waits, the goal pill reads "accept your SK" and a tap brings the card back.
- Upgrading the last piece of a combo, such as a Warung to level 3 beside a Kebun Sayur, now shows the combo card at once.

See `decisions/sk-ceremony-combo-upgrade.md`.
