Pak Pejabat: Privacy Policy

Bahasa Indonesia di bawah

English

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Pak Pejabat collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

  • Your game progress and settings are saved on your device only (browser storage). We never receive them.
  • The game does not send your data to any server. It only downloads its own files from hurrymeow.github.io, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP address under its own privacy statement.
  • We share nothing with third parties.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes your progress.

The game is satire for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hurrymeow/lurahsimulator.

Game ini satire untuk dewasa (18+) dan tidak ditujukan untuk anak-anak.
