# Menara Wifi level 1: a mast, not a hammer

Review (2026-11-08): level 1 "looks too simple and looks like a hammer" (a yellow pole with a crossbar on top). It is now an RT/RW net mast: a grey pipe on a concrete foot held by three guy wires, two sector panels, a grid dish and a router box with its cable. It stays shorter than the level 2 lattice tower, so the blinking light in `map.ts` sits lower at level 1.
