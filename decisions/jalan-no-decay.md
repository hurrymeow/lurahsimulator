# Jalan Setapak no longer breaks

- **Paths never crack, spread or need repair.** Hari found it annoying. The monthly decay system, the tile/stretch/favour repair buttons and the potholes art are gone.
- **Floods already skip paths** (they count as cover), so nothing else breaks a path.
- **Old saves heal on load.** Any `damaged` Jalan Setapak, including AI neighbours', is set back to `active` and loses its `crackedAt`.
- Docs: GAME-DESIGN.md and ART.md no longer describe broken paths. `decisions/path-decay-levels-terraces.md` is superseded for the decay part.
