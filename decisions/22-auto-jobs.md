- **Residents pick their own jobs:** each month a resident with no job rolls a weighted random pick among jobs with an open slot, weighted by `appeal` in `data/jobs.ts` (PNS 20, Influencer 50, Ojol 90, Buzzer 100). If no slot is open they stay unemployed. The roll hashes the month and resident id instead of drawing from the game's random stream, so job picks never shift event or arrival timing (drawing from it broke the star-pacing tests on seeds 2 and 7). A pick shows a small pop on the job's building. Any manual choice in Warga, including "No job", sets `manual` on that resident and the sim never touches the job again. A resident who retires (PNS) or is exposed (Buzzer) also stays unemployed until the player acts, so they do not rejoin the same job at once. Residents who lose a slot to a demolished building may pick again.
