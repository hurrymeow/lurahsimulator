# Ease crowding and show the population cap

- Walkers drop from 16 to 10. Employed residents (not ojol, not the inspector) leave by the ring road in daylight and are hidden until evening, then re-enter at an edge tile.
- The Pop chip turns amber, shows "!" and pulses when population reaches housing. Tapping it says to build another house.
- Residents page drops the job slot summary for one short hint line.
- Speech bubbles: 12% chance (was 55%), 1.8s, smaller font, one on screen at a time, no repeat of the last line, 6-7 lines per building (was 2-3).
