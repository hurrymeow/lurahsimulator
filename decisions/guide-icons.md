# Guide pictures say it in words

The How to play and tip picture rows used the people glyph, which reads as nothing ("what is this icon?"). The beds, Resident and ranking rows now use short words in EN and ID next to the building sprites. Resource icons that match the top bar stay.

## Second pass
Every picture row was reviewed. Rows that leaned on an icon nobody could read (the people glyph, the permit and biopori sprites, the wifi sprite that looks like a hammer, the Virality icon) now use words: Menu, Build, Permit, Resident, Job, Biopori, Road, Combo, with EN and ID labels. Kept: building sprites that read at a glance (Warung, Pos Ronda, Kantor RT, Indomaret, Parkir, Rumah) and the top-bar resource icons.
The pixel heading font draws a lowercase f like a capital A, so "Residents find their own work" read as "Residents And their own work". The two headings with an f are reworded, and a unit test keeps f out of How to play headings. Other pixel headings (event and sheet titles) may have the same glyph and are not changed here.
