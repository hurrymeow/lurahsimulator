# T8: item 12 counts reward kinds by category

Item 12 used to count a star as two kinds when it had any unlock plus a resource reward, so a star that opened a shop, a decor and a farm read as one. It now counts distinct members of four kinds per star: non-decor building (`def(id).category !== 'decoration'`), decor, resource reward (`star.reward` has a key), and new system. The pass bar keeps the 30s affordability check and adds "every star bundles 2+ kinds" and "a new system at least every 2 stars" (GAME-DESIGN.md section 8 item 12).

How the new-system kind was found, so the rule can be re-checked. I read `StarDef` (`src/sim/types.ts`), the star unlocks in `src/data/stages.ts` and `src/data/higher/stages.ts`, and the content those ids open: `STAGE_METERS` and `STAGE_JOBS` gates (`src/data/higher/index.ts`), staff roles with their `opens` stage and stars (`src/data/staff.ts`), and stage buildings with an `fx` block (`stageFx` in `src/sim/content.ts`). I then grepped `src/sim` for `stage.stars` comparisons to find hand-coded stage flags; the only one that opens a system at a star is `boardSystem`, which starts the Camat board at RT 1★ (`src/sim/board.ts:111`), so it is the one entry in `FLAG_SYSTEMS`. A system counts at the star where its gate first opens (`stars === k + 1`); stage-entry systems gated at 0 stars are promotion content, not a star reward. Event `minStars` and building/upgrade `requires.stars` were checked and excluded: they gate ordinary content, not a new system. `PATH_UNLOCKS` was left out because item 12 reads `star.unlocks`, so Kel 2★'s path buildings (Terminal, Taman) are not counted; no stage data was changed.
