# Guide narrator voice and placement

Hari found the opener abrupt and its box far from the Menu button.

- **Narrator voice.** The opener opens with "Hi, welcome, Pak Pejabat! This is your area. Let's start by building a house. Tap the Menu button below." in EN and ID. Each later step is one warm sentence, and the last one hands over to time and the goal.
- **No card before it.** A new game with the guide skips the first-launch How to play card. Time is taught in the closing note. Without the guide (`?debug`, old saves) the card still shows.
- **Time holds on the welcome** until the first tap, so a month never passes under the greeting.
- **Placement.** The note sits right above the bottom bar, with an arrow to Menu. Inside a sheet it sits right under the target, or above it when there is no room. It lets taps through, so it never blocks a tile.
- **Tips** sit low, near the thumb, not in the middle of the map.
- **Tone pass.** Every How to play card and tip is rewritten in the same voice, EN and ID. The Banjir and report card tips have new headings.
