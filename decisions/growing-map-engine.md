# Growing map engine

- **Frame-rate gate passed, after a fix.** A packed 36x52 map ran at 5 fps against 22 fps for a packed 9x13 map, at 6x CPU throttle. The cause was per-frame work over every tile: a scan of all buildings for each empty tile (about 1.7M checks a frame) and a full-size blit of the world canvas. Tile lookups are now sets, off-screen tiles and buildings are skipped, and only the visible slice of the world canvas is drawn. The same map now runs at about 70% of the small map's frame rate, and 52x68 costs the same as 36x52. The new E2E test keeps that ratio above 50% (best of three, 6x throttle).
- **Grid size follows the stage.** `GRID_W` and `GRID_H` are live bindings that `setGrid(stage)` changes: 9x13 for RT, 18x26 for RW, 36x52 for Kelurahan. One map instance at a time makes this safe, and it avoids passing the size through every renderer function.
- **The player's RT never moves.** It is always the top-left cell, so promotion shifts no building and no save needs migrating. Old saves get an empty neighbour list and open as before.
- **Seam lots are packed dirt, two tiles wide.** RW buildings go on the seams inside the player's RW, Kelurahan buildings on the seam between RWs. RT buildings stay inside the RT, so a few edge tiles are lost to new RT builds after promotion. Existing buildings there stay. The player cannot build in an AI RT; tapping one opens the Neighbours panel.
- **AI RTs are separate state, not extra buildings.** Their buildings, residents and stars live in `GameState.neighbours`, so the player's income, combos, jobs and ranking never read them. A neighbour is viewed as a small game state of its own so the player's scoring, combo and star code run on it unchanged. That is what "same rules as the player" means here.
- **One Dana pays for one build.** It costs Rp 1jt (the RW data's `danaPerBuild`), and an RT banks at most 3. An unfunded RT visibly stalls. AI RTs build every 3 months (Rajin every 2, Hobi Wacana skips every third turn, Jarang Muncul sends 2x letters).
- **AI RT Sabar is modelled, not simulated.** It is 45 plus 5 per social building (pos ronda, wifi, selokan), capped at 80. Ranking wins use the same score formula against the rival curve without jitter.
- **Kas RW.** Every AI RT pays iuran into the player's kas each month, resident count times the usual iuran, Rajin 20% more.
- **Rival moves up.** Kampung Sebelah, RW Sebelah, Kelurahan Sebelah. The area score is the player's RT plus every AI RT in it. The rival curve is scaled 1x, 2.6x and 8x, because AI RTs score below the player. These two numbers are a first guess for the balance autoplayer to tune.
- **Stempel is in, Anggaran is not.** The RW's first star needs the Stempel backlog under 5 for 6 months, so the meter had to exist. Stamps refill by 3 a month plus 1 per Balai RW, letters are stamped automatically, and each letter left waiting costs 0.5 Sabar. There is no stamp button yet. Pressure only shows with many funded RTs, which is the Kelurahan case.
- **RW is not playable yet.** The SK hook is wired and RW stars count real AI state, but RW buildings, combos, jobs and events are still data with no engine effects. `playable` stays off until the content PR.
- **Ranking results carry a tier.** Old results count as the RT's, so `tierWins` stays honest after promotion.
- **Scenery thins at RT 2★.** Roofs of the neighbours show beyond the ring road. No extra scenery cost, they join the existing back-to-front list.
