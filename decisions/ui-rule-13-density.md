# UI rule 13: little text at a time

Date: 2026-10-31. Approved by Hari.

- New UI.md rule 13: at most 50 words in view per screen, 20 per block, 300 per sheet; at most 2 levels below the map; 1 primary button per screen. List length is not capped.
- Why: Hari wants menus as simple as possible, with little text at a time. No published guideline gives a words-per-screen number, so the bars follow from reading speed (about 238 wpm, Brysbaert 2019), skim rate (about 20%, NN/g) and NN/g's two-level limit for progressive disclosure.
- Measured 2026-10-31 at 360px on the Kelurahan seed: 5 of 11 screens fail (Neighbours, How-to card, Wacana, Stats, Build tabs). The fix is queued in TODO.md; Hari asked for TODO first.
- Against: word counts reward cutting words, not clarity. The UI checklist's first-click test remains the check on meaning.
