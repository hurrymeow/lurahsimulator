# Permit and build phases on the map

- Before, a permit-waiting building drew as a 35% faded building and a building under way as a 50% faded building in scaffolding. The two looked alike at phone size.
- Now the permit phase draws only a staked plot (white string on red-tipped stakes) under the permit sign, so it reads as "not started". The tap target is the plot.
- The build phase keeps the faded building and scaffolding and adds a yellow tower crane. Mangkrak keeps scaffolding with no crane, so a stalled site looks abandoned.
- Badges sit on the top of what is drawn: the crane while building, the plot while waiting for a permit.
