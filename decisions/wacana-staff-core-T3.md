The PIC model gives every Wacana and Realisasi a lead from the player's staff, preselected as the best free person for Gaul or Rajin and always including Inem. Tuning lives in `PIC` in `src/data/staff.ts`: Gaul scales a wacana's power from ×0.5 at 0, Rajin scales a Realisasi's delay from ×1.5 at 0 with a 1-week floor, a busy lead works at half output, and Jujur below 60 carries up to a 30% chance to lose 20% of the budget. Inem can lead many programs at once; everyone else leads one until it lands.
