## What holds Viral up
Seven bot types at Kelurahan 3★ (seeds 1 and 2) all end at Viral 85 to 89. Each holds 12 to 16 Influencers, which is every Influencer slot. Auto-assign fills the slots, so no choice is involved. Over a 48-week year the hint path gains about 340 from jobs, 70 from combos and 30 from income buildings. The cap taper (`gain.ts`) and the 10% weekly decay settle it near 85.

## Why the high level is not a problem
- Judol starts at 1% a week per 10 Viral above 20 and quits at 2% a week. At Viral 85 that settles at 75% of households; at 60, 67%; at 40, 50%.
- Viral raises shop income by 0.5% a point. Judol takes 30% of income times its share. Net shop income: **x1.10** at Viral 85, **x1.04** at 60, **x1.02** at 40.
- So past 60, extra Viral is worth about 6% of shop income. Judol already cancels it. That is the joke in the design (GAME-DESIGN.md §2, Iklan Bertarget).
- Cutting Viral supply would move every Act 1 star time, since Viral adds 40% to shop income before the Kecamatan. That re-measure is not worth a plateau that costs 6%.

## Where play does move judol
Razia (half the cases move away), Literasi Keuangan (stops new cases), fewer Baliho. These work in the Kecamatan as designed.
