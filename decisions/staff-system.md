# Staff: hiring, rarity, management and Inem's dream (Proposal 3)

Julian's design review, approved by Hari on 2026-10-28, with his answers: a cap of 10 managed by hand; a Lowongan through Grup WA RT or Online; 2-3★ common, 4★ advanced, 5★ rare, 6★ legendary, with no 5★ or 6★ in the kampung act, for base stats only; Inem as permanent staff number 1, her dream as sub-goals.

- **Why a staff layer.** Auto-jobs fixed the tedium of assigning warga but left nobody to care about. Kairosoft games pair guests who live on their own with staff you manage (Game Dev Story 4 to 8, Mega Mall Story 4, Doraemon Dorayaki Shop 6). The late-game boredom of a small cap is answered by mutasi: trained staff leave to run a neighbouring RT, so the player keeps hiring and training.
- **Inem counts in the 10** and holds the Asisten role. Her wage is Rp 75k a month: at Rp 250k the balance bot stalled at 22 residents, since it was half an early kampung's income.
- **Grup WA vs Online is a real choice.** Grup WA brings your own jobless warga (a hired one counts as in work for Kerja, and a preman applicant comes as Mantan Preman); Online brings better odds but outsiders.
- **Specials are types, not people** (GAME-DESIGN.md section 5: no real politicians or officials; no single real person). Portraits for them are a later art pass; until then a card shows a star badge, and Inem shows her portrait.
- **What is not built yet:** the Admin Grup WA's "bad news lands softer" and the Supir's Kunjungan warning (the role gives crew speed for now); 5★ and 6★ odds wait for Acts 2 and 3; the later gigs wait for those acts.
- Rolls hash the seed, month and ids, so staff never shift the shared random stream. Old saves get Inem on load.
- Balance: with Inem's wage the bot's 2★ moves from about month 22 to 31 to 36 and 3★ to about 51; all pacing targets still pass.
