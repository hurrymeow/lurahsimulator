# Inem only on events; warga without stars

Julian's playtest notes, 2026-10-28.

- **Inem showed up too often.** Her portrait sat on every pop-up: tips, unlocks, the meter card, star fanfares, events. She now comes only with events (the event card and its result card) and her own news (gig notices, via `inem: true` on a notice), plus the opener. Everything else is a plain card again.
- **Warga have no rating.** The Warga list showed each resident's job level as 1 to 5 stars, with a By level filter (from #116). Now that staff carry star rarity, the same mark meant two things, and it invited managing warga one by one. The stars and the By level filter are gone. The experience bonus stays (levels at 6, 18, 36 and 60 months, +20 to 25% output each, a bigger pension for PNS) and shows as a line in a resident's own sheet: "18 months in this job: output +40%. More in 18 months." The map pop says "has more experience" instead of "Lv 3".
