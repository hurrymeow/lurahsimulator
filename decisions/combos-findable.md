# Combos easy to find

- Buku Combo now shows every combo's name, buildings, distance and effect from the start. Only the story waits for discovery.
- Each combo shows a status: active in town, found but not working now, or not in town yet with the missing buildings.
- The star checklist lists each combo under the combo goal, with the same conditions and status.
- Why: playtest said finding combos by hint was frustrating.
