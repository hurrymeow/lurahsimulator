# Environment art refresh (painterly ground, Pokemon-style outlines, weather)

- **Painterly environment (autumn arena and Octopath field references):** object designs stay the same, palms included. Ground gets clumped grass, packed dirt with pebbles, sand ripples, a shimmering sea and scattered fallen leaves, all baked into the static world canvas, so it costs nothing per frame. The kampung is a raised block with dirt cliffs down to the beach, for diorama depth. The sky is deep blue down to warm haze, with soft slab clouds. Mountains get straight strata and forested feet, and stop at the horizon. A pine tree line runs along the horizon. Sprites get roof tile courses, plank or bark lines and leaf sprigs.
- **Coloured outlines (HGSS/DPPt reference):** every outline, on walkers, buildings, trees, props and the sun, is a dark shade of the colour it borders instead of flat ink. Silhouette edges use 0.45x of the touching fill, face edges 0.55x of their own colour. Flat ink stays only for eyes, the sun's face and shadows.
- **Walkers:** more detail at the same 7x10 size: hair with a shine in one of five colours, eyes, collar, belt, shading derived from the shirt. Shirt colour still carries the job.
- **Terrain borders:** black ink lines are replaced by a dark edge in the higher ground's own colour, plus a bright rim on the lower side that eases back over three pixels. Sea meets sand with foam. The vignette keeps the screen edges darkest.
- **Weather:** a random rain shower every 60 to 150 s, lasting 15 to 30 s: up to 80 slanted streaks, ground ripples and an 18% cool dim. Waves splash on the visible shore all the time. Effects only. A full shower measured 77 to 89% of the effects-off frame rate at 6x CPU throttle. The CI frame test never meets a shower, so drops were capped at 80 for headroom.
