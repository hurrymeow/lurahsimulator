# Combo range edge to edge, and a moved building keeps its look

- **Combo range is counted between lot edges, not top corners.** A new test builds every combo in the book (RT, RW and Kelurahan) on legal lots, then moves a member away and back. It found that Agustusan Akbar (Gapura + Lapangan, radius 2) could never fire. The Gapura stands on an RW seam, the 3x3 Lapangan on an RT lot, and no pair of legal lots put their top corners within 2 tiles of each other (0 of 116). Counting from the nearest edges gives 60 layouts that work. For two one-tile buildings nothing changes. A 2x2 or 3x3 member now counts as close as its footprint looks, so a few big-building combos fire a little more easily. Still Chebyshev, as `decisions/content-decor-biopori.md` says.
- **Wisata Kolam names Rumah Makan.** The retired combo still asked for a `gerobakBakso`, but a Gerobak Bakso counts as a level 1 Rumah Makan since `decisions/content-brands.md`, so the combo could never fire, even in old saves. It now needs Kolam Ikan Hias + Rumah Makan and boosts Rumah Makan x1.8.
- **One look for map and ghost.** `MapView.look(b)` gives sprite, variant, footprint, level and turn. The map draws with it and the move ghost copies it, so the two cannot drift apart again. The ghost also draws the building's level pips while it is carried.
- **`?debug` exposes `pakrtDefs`** (the building list) beside `pakrt`, so e2e tests can loop over every building.
