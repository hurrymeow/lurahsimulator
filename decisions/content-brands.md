# Content trim, problem unlocks, brands (Proposal 2)

Julian's design review, approved by Hari on 2026-10-28. The RT opened 14 buildings at month 1 and 32 by the first star, most of them saying "earns, and calms people down", so none had a reason.

- **Rumah Makan** merges Gerobak Bakso, Warkop and Warteg as levels 1 to 3 (Rp 800k, Rp 150k a month at level 1, instant). Level 1 and 3 use the artist's cart and warteg sprites; level 2 is a lower warkop drawn beside the artist's, so the step stays within one storey (`tests/art.test.ts`).
- **Warung Sembako** is the warung, renamed for the coming Perut need. Toko Kelontong is its level 3.
- **Retired, not deleted.** Gerobak Bakso, Warkop, Warteg, Toko Kelontong, Pot Bunga, Kolam Ikan Hias, Taman Bermain and Air Mancur can no longer be built. Old saves keep theirs working. Combos count a retired building as the one it joined, at the level it stood for (Warteg = Rumah Makan level 3). Wisata Kolam, Taman Keluarga and Jalan Santai are retired combos: they still fire in old saves, and the Buku Combo lists them only once found.
- **Combos can ask for a level** (`'rumahMakan@3'`). Nine combos were remapped; their effect lines say Rumah Makan or Warung Sembako.
- **Decor 9 to 5**, each with its own job: Pohon Rindang +0.3 Popularitas a month (no income aura now), Balai-balai Bambu (Bangku Taman renamed, +6% within 2 tiles), Lampu Jalan (Lampu Taman renamed; it gains Aman in Proposal 1), Mural +1 Viral a month (no income aura now), Patung Ikon (opens at RW).
- **Unlock pacing.** Start: 6 buildings. Problem unlocks: Selokan after the first banjir, Gudang Material and Kebun Sayur when 3 warga go 6 months without work, Fotokopi when a permit queue forms. Then 7 at 1 star, 6 at 2 stars, 5 at 3 stars, and Kontrakan, Kolam Lele and Patung Ikon at RW. A test checks the first-star menu stays at 16 or fewer.
- **Jobless means idle for half a year,** counted per resident (`Resident.idle`). The four starting residents have no workplaces yet, so counting jobless at once opened the farms in month 1. Proposal 1 reuses the counter for preman.
- **Old saves keep their menu:** a save without `problems` counts every problem whose building it had already unlocked or built.
- **Brands, only for chains,** acting like the chain does: minimarkets (IndoApril, BetaMart, now 2 allowed each) make Rivalitas Abadi with each other and Kanibal (-15%) with their own kind within 2 tiles; wifi towers carry a provider, and two different providers within 2 tiles make the new **Perang Kuota** combo; Rumah Makan, Apotek, Bengkel and Barbershop are franchises (+8% per sister outlet, up to +24%, 5% royalty added to the Setoran). A brand comes from the uid, so old saves get one without touching the shared RNG; the inspector can change it. Price wars stay on minimarkets and wifi only, so the joke does not go stale.
- **Names.** Non-chains get a local name (Warung Bu Sari, Kos Pak Joko, Fotokopi Berkah Jaya Abadi). Any building can be renamed in the inspector, up to 24 characters; a rename never changes the brand. Names are proper names, the same in both languages.
- **Event spacing fix.** The bot now reaches the first star by month 3 (it was 9), because its money no longer goes on the population-6 shops. That let Harga Cabai land two months before Gunting Pita: `crowded()` only looked ahead to yearly events. It now also sees one-off scheduled events.
- **Balance bot.** It keeps the three tiles it saves for its combo free, and restarts a stalled project with Orang Inside when it has spare, as a player would. Pacing: 1 star by month 3, 2 stars by 22 to 36, 3 stars by 31 to 36 across the 10 seeds; all targets pass.
