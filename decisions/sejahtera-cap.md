# Sejahtera cap for a tiny kampung

Sejahtera (the 2045 meter) now has a ceiling that grows with residents: 30 at none, 100 at 25 or more. Perut and Kerja read 100 when nobody needs feeding or work, so an empty kampung looked like Pahlawan. The cap is carried on `Meters` as an optional `cap`, so old saves have no ceiling. Follows the docs. Source: `decisions/kelurahan-playtest.md`, finding 4.
