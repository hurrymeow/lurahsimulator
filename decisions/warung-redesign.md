# Warung Sembako redesign

2026-11-02. The owner asked for a Warung Sembako that reads more obviously as a warung. Three designs were rendered, each with three levels: a gerobak (kiosk cart), a tenda (kiosk camp) and a kios kelontong (window kiosk). The owner mixed them:

- Level 1: the tenda, with the gerobak's red-and-white striped canopy colours on its terpal.
- Level 2: the gerobak parked under a zinc lean-to with a plank back wall, a galon, an LPG melon and a bench.
- Level 3: the kios kelontong's brick toko under a flat dak, with a big yellow nameboard and a blue rolling door half up.

Every level hangs rentengan sachet strips along the front, the clearest "warung" sign at map scale. The art moved to `src/render/warung-art.ts`. ART.md, Building levels, describes the three looks. Each level stays on its one tile and within the height rules.
