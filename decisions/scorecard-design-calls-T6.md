# T6: item 15 times the dearest key item and the growth ratio

Task T6 of the scorecard design calls. Item 15's "next key item" was the cheapest unowned non-decor kind, which the builder can always afford at once, so the row measured the wrong thing. `Row.dearKey` now records the dearest non-decor, non-housing kind the player could build now and does not own (the same filter as `nextKeyCost`, with `Math.max`), and item 15 times that instead; item 14 keeps `nextKey`. The row also computes, per stage, the median `net` over the median cost of that stage's star unlocks, and requires income growth over cost growth to sit within 0.7 to 1.5 from RT to RW and RW to Kelurahan. Since the previously unmeasured half is now measured, the row uses `verdict`, not `partial`. A local builder run on seeds 1 to 3 gives a median 27s and p95 109s for the dearest item, but growth of 6.73x from RT to RW (and 1.11x from RW to Kelurahan), so the row now fails on income outrunning costs in the RT-to-RW step. That is a game-balance result from the new measure, not a harness error; no `src/` data changed.
