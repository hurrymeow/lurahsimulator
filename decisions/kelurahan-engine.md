# Kelurahan engine

- **Serapan is measured against what is due.** The yearly budget is spread over 12 months, and serapan is the spend so far as a percent of the budget due so far. Spending the whole year up front keeps it above 100% for the year. A flat 80% of the full budget would have failed everyone every February.
- **All spending counts.** Builds, upgrades, events, wacana and repairs all raise `stats.spent`. Aspal Ulang and Studi Banding stay as the cheap way to hit the number with no map effect.
- **Unspent money cuts the next budget by the same share, floor 20%.** A bad year can't zero the next one. The first budget arrives on the first month in the stage.
- **The December rush spends half the shortfall** if the kas can cover it. A full rush would make the unspent cut impossible to feel.
- **Dana Desa halves without a Pendamping Desa,** on the Desa path from 2★, when that job exists. The job is also the only Rp 150.000 a month it earns.
- **A wacana has a receipt for 12 months after it is announced.** Older ones have none. Tim Audit's free choice costs 2 Patience per receipt-less wacana, and counts as a clean audit only when there are none. The paid choice is always clean.
- **Lomba Kelurahan multiplies the area score for the months the data says (1).** It only helps if the yearly ranking falls in that month. Left as the data has it.
- **Panen Raya pays each October.** Rp 2.000.000 per standing combo.
- **Honorer** holds 2 desks at the Kantor or Balai Desa, is never taken for pension, and has a 20% a year chance of becoming PNS after 60 months of work, if a PNS desk is free.
- **Perang Tarif lifts ojol income x1.3** next to Terminal's x1.3. **Spot Instagramable doubles Taman upkeep.** **Embung** adds 30% to banjir protection, inside the 80% cap.
- **Not built here:** 2x2 footprints belong to the build-mode thread. The half-size zoom, the Desa path pick and the `playable` flip come after, so Kelurahan stays unreachable.
