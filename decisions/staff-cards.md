# Staff page: one card per person, one main action

Hari found the Staff page overwhelming: every person showed Bimtek, Mutasi and Let go, and every open role showed one post button per channel. Kairosoft's staff screens show one card per person and one main action.

- Each staff card shows face or stars, name, level, role and salary, plus any warning (away, raise refused). It has one main button: "Give the raise" when they asked for one, otherwise Bimtek. Inem has none.
- The card's name block is a 48px button that opens the person's detail view: role blurb, trait, stat bars, the raise with Refuse, Bimtek, Mutasi and Let go. A "Back to Staff" button returns to the list.
- Posting a lowongan is one "Post a lowongan" button per open role. The channels, with price and blurb, sit in the next view.
- Applicants keep one Hire button each, and the waiting lowongan keeps its Cancel button: both are already single actions.
- Built without a layout draft from Hari, at the owner's request. Hari reviews it in the PR.
