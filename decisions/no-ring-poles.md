# No poles on the ring road

2026-11-02. From a playtest screenshot, the owner asked to remove the electric poles round the ring road. Poles now stand only inside the kampung, beside the jalan setapak (one every four to ten path tiles in build order), each wired to the nearest earlier path pole within nine tiles. The first pole has nothing to wire to until a second goes up. The ring-road pole loop and its spans are removed from `src/render/cables.ts`. ART.md's Poles and cables row is updated.
