# UI hierarchy: control roles, Wacana list, setup screen

Date: 2026-11-01. Decided by Hari ("do b, and yes to the rest").

- Wacana becomes a list of policies with a detail pop-up. Kerjakan (Realisasi) is the primary, Umumkan the secondary. The pop-up stays open after announcing.
- New UI.md rule 14: primary, secondary, choice and input each have their own look. Only the primary uses the accent fill. Disabled keeps the role's shape.
- CI enforces rule 13 word counts and the one-primary limit. Known fails become a capped allowlist.
- Setup screen: plain heading, Pak/Bu as a choice segment, dice icon for random, no repeated "Kampung" prefix, Kembali as secondary.
- Perut, Kerja and Aman get stat icons.

Why: Hari found the Wacana sheet too texty with no hierarchy, and the setup screen "all buttons look the same". Rule 13 already listed Wacana as failing, but nothing enforced it, and UI.md had no rule on how roles look.
