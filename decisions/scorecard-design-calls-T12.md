# T12: item 24 old map keeps paying

Item 24's second half is now measured in the sim instead of being left to T2. At each promotion `runBot` records the uids of the buildings standing then; 38 ticks later (the first tick at or after 5 min of wall time, at 8s a tick) it sums `buildingIncome` of those uids that are still on the map and logs an `oldMap` event with the total. The row passes only when every promotion in every builder seed has a positive sum and a reading exists (a run that ends before the 5 min mark leaves the promotion unread and fails). The old "Whether the old map stays useful is T2" text is gone and the row uses `verdict`, not `partial`. A synthetic 10-seed test in `harness.test.ts` covers pass, a quiet old map, an unread promotion and a seed that never promotes.
