# Builder bots spend Orang Dalam on Kunjungan

Task T3 of `docs/plans/scorecard-leftovers.md`. The builder and shopkeeper plans now spend spare Orang Dalam on a neighbour visit. `visitNext` in `tests/scorecard/driver.ts` returns early unless `s.res.orangDalam >= NEIGHBOUR.visitOd`, then visits the first AI-run RT whose `checkVisit` says yes (`visit` itself still enforces the Orang Dalam cost, the 12-week cooldown and a non-full lot) and counts it as one action. A `visit` flag on `Plan` turns it on for those two plans only, so the other bots' plans stay as they were. The intent is the one in the plan's decision 3: the scorecard should not read a bot gap where a real player would spend the currency.

Item 13 of `tests/scorecard/scorecard.ts` has no hardcoded bot-gap line to delete: its evidence is computed from the timelines, so the phrases disappear on their own when the column moves. A local builder run of 10 seeds at the scorecard settings (`runBot('builder', seed, 600, { stopStars: 9 })`) gives the `odn` column a fall in B4 on seeds 4, 6 and 7 (ticks 260 to 312), so "Orang Dalam B4 never spent" drops. B1 still never falls: the builder is still in the RT stage until about 13.5 min, while B1 ends at 5 min, and `newGame` has no neighbours to visit yet. The B1 phrase therefore stays, as the task allows, and `scorecard.ts` was left unchanged. The same run also reaches B5 on a few seeds, where item 13 reports its own real finding.
