# One isometric world: volcanoes on the island, open sea, no sky

2026-11-02. Playtest feedback: the painted twin mountains and the sun between them did not match the isometric map, the back two edges of the island ended in a straight cut into the sea, and the few sea islands all looked alike.

Three options were rendered side by side (volcanoes in the island's terrain mesh; no horizon, open sea; or the backdrop kept but redrawn in 2:1 facets). The owner chose a mix of the first two.

- **No horizon, no sky.** An isometric view has no vanishing point, so the sea now runs past every edge of the world and the whole island shows as one diorama block. The golden-hour sky, cloud banks, horizon tree line and far-island silhouettes are gone.
- **Volcanoes are terrain.** The twin volcanoes are part of the hills' corner-height mesh (`VOLCS` and `cornerHeight` in `src/render/scenery.ts`), so they take the same lit triangle facets and tile grid line, and a soft maximum joins them to the hills so the skirts flow into the slopes. This took three rounds with the owner: the first mesh version kept round, streaky cones; stacked isometric blocks (whole tiles, then half tiles with dithering) read as isometric but broke from the terrain round them, so the owner asked for slopes shaped like the terrain. What stayed from those rounds: the forest, rock and grey `abu` (ash) tiers are picked per pixel by height and blend through the 4x4 dither (ART.md's dithering rule covers the volcanoes for this), and the cone's texture is sampled on screen so steep faces are not streaked. Only a few trees stand on the gentle forest belt, and a tree a slope would hide is left out, because trees draw after the baked world and would show through it.
- **The sun is removed.** With no sky there is nowhere for it to stand; the key light from the top left stays in the cel shading and god rays. The sun's face, its halo and its bob-and-blink loop go with it. ART.md no longer lists the sun as the face that earns the joke.
- **Organic coast.** The island is no longer a diamond. One coast function (`coastAt`) gives every side a line that wanders in bays and headlands of a few tiles and rounds the four corners, with a beach and foam all the way round; the back edges no longer end in a straight cut. The front sides only wander outward, so the sawah, the ring road and the neighbours' roofs never fall into the sea. The world grew 3 tiles on its front sides so the coast never meets the canvas edge.
- **Islands.** 15 to 18 islands (capped, so the Kecamatan map does not fill with them) in four size bands, from rocks to big hilly islands. Each is a small corner-height mesh on half-tile facets, painted with the ground's own materials and borders, ringed with foam, with a wet-sand bank on the near side and the map's own trees. No perspective shrink and no haze, since the projection has none.
- **No aerial perspective.** The far-land haze, the hill-silhouette fray and the far tree clumps and hamlets are removed. Clouds are the only dithered thing left.
- World build time is unchanged or a little faster (measured at RW, Kelurahan and Kecamatan sizes): the sea is filled only where no ground lies, and islands share one rank buffer.

Docs updated: ART.md (geometry, outlines, palette, scenery table, rules, lighting layers, motion, faces, terraced hills) and GAME-DESIGN.md section 6 art lines.
- **Gradual rise from the shore.** Three slope options were rendered (long concave aprons, coastal benches, one massif); the owner chose the aprons. The volcano bases are wider with a long concave profile, and both the hills and the volcanoes ease up from the beach over a few tiles, so the land climbs from the sand to the crater with no flat ledge. The island gained 4 tiles of land on its back sides to make room for the climb.
- **Woods and smaller trees.** The owner asked for most of the green land to be wooded and the trees scaled to 70%. Scenery trees are now drawn from the same sprites resampled once to 70% on whole art pixels, so the view's whole-number scale rule still holds; ART.md records them as the one smaller sprite, with trunks about 3px instead of 4. Up to three trees stand on a tile across forest, kebun, open grass, the tea hills (sparser) and the volcanoes' forest belt, and the islands are wooded the same way. Sawah, terraces, rock and steep faces stay open. The frame-rate test still passes (Kelurahan at 0.44 of RT, floor 0.4), but the extra trees cost about 20% of frames on both maps under the 6x throttle.
- **Karst knolls and tree sizes.** The owner asked for rocky hills round the kampung so the green land is not mostly empty, trees at their feet, and random tree sizes except palms. Limestone karst knolls (after Gunung Kidul) now ring the kampung past the ring road, front sawah included, so ART.md's rule that the front sawah stays flat now reads "flat but for its karst knolls"; the beach cliff, plots and taps are untouched. A new `batu` material shows on their steep facets. Each knoll has a tree ring at its foot, thick behind and beside it and thin on the side facing the viewer so the rock shows. Scenery trees other than palms now take their own size from 50% to 90%; palms stay at 70%. Knolls are indexed per tile so the world still builds in about the same time (Kecamatan 3.1s against 2.7s before this work).
- **Smaller, less bubbly trees, hutan sawit, poles and cables.** The owner asked for all trees at half size with sizes still varied, a less bubbly shade tree, oil-palm plantations round the volcanoes' feet, and option A of the kampung ideas (poles and messy cables). Scenery trees are now native sprites (`src/render/scenery-trees.ts`) at 25% to 45% of a buildable tree, palms 35%: shrinking the buildable sprites to that size broke their outlines. The shade tree is a layered canopy tree. Up to five stand on a tile. Plantation blocks sit on the lower aprons in straight rows with dirt tracks. Poles and cables run round the ring road (`src/render/cables.ts`), each span a cached sprite, with the kit growing by stage. To hold the frame-rate test at 320px, scenery trees more than two tiles from the ring road are baked into the world canvas and no longer sway; the test passes at 320 and 412px (Kelurahan at 0.44 to 0.57 of RT). Drop lines to individual houses are left for later, since houses move and a cable ending in mid-air reads wrong.
- **Poles by the paths, isometric canopy, plantation fix.** The owner asked for more poles inside the kampung than on the ring road, beside the jalan setapak every two to five squares, with new paths raising new poles. Path poles are derived from the paths in build order (no save change), so they follow the player's paths exactly and vanish when a building replaces a path; the ring road's spacing went from three to five tiles. The canopy tree looked flattened, so each tier is now a disc in the map's projection: a 2:1 top face with a shaded side band, and the crown about as tall as wide. The oil palms were too small and spilled out of their blocks, some onto the cone: they are now one full-grown palm per tile, centred, and blocks only go on gentle tiles low on the aprons, with a clearing round them.
- **Fewer poles, ragged road edge, crisp trees, island hills.** From a playtest screenshot: poles halved (four to ten path tiles inside, every ten tiles on the ring road); the ring road's outer edge is ragged and the tree line wanders, so the border between the road and the woods is no longer a ruled line (ART.md's "the ring road keeps exact tile edges" now holds for its inner edge only); blurry trees came from fractional positions smoothed when the far woods were baked, so trees now stand on whole art pixels and bake without smoothing; and big islands carry a small volcano or a cluster of karst knolls, middling ones sometimes a knoll or two, on lower island land so the hills give the height.
