# Lomba Agustusan, the big yearly event

Julian picked idea 1 from the TODO, 2026-10-29. It replaces the one-tap "17 Agustus: Lomba" event.

- **Shape:** a prep window (from May, a card that opens the panel), then the day (August) as a full-screen flow. Each contest is a page with its own tap, so it cannot be dismissed in one tap. Two contests are short mini-games: Panjat Pinang is timing (3 grips before 3 slips), and Balap Karung is mashing (30 taps in 5 s). Two are decided by the odds, which are shown first, so a player who does not want mini-games can still hold a lomba.
- **Stats used:** the ketua panitia's Rajin (grip band) and Gaul (fewer taps, better odds). The kampung's Aman (odds; ricuh below 40) and Popularitas (turnout).
- **Reuses:** Lapangan and the 17 Agustus combos still multiply the prizes (`agustusMult`). Inem's Panggung 17 Agustus gig completes when she headlines the dangdut stage. Her old route (Lapangan and Popularitas 50 in August) still works.
- **Costs and prizes:** contests 300rb to 1,2jt, stages up to 3,5jt. A full-effort year (3 wins, dangdut stage, Lapangan, Popularitas 60) returns about Viral +43, Popularitas +36 and Rp 3,8jt. The old event with a Lapangan gave Popularitas +30 and Viral +20 for Rp 1jt, so the new one pays a little more for much more effort and money.
- **From year 2:** year 1 is for learning, as the old event also waited.
- **Spacing:** random events keep 3 months clear of the day, as they did of the old scheduled event.
- **Trophy:** a small gold cup drawn on the Kantor RT for 12 months after a clean sweep of 2 or more. It is new 12x12 art in the sign set (`piala`).
- **Reduce motion:** the climb marker moves at half speed. There are no other animations.
- Not done: a 3-month build-up of stage props on the map, and festival walkers. Possible follow-ups.
