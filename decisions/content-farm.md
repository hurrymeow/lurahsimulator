# Farmland and the Petani job (playtest feedback)

- Four farm buildings: Kebun Sayur, Sawah, Kandang Ayam, Kolam Lele. Each earns on its own and opens a farm job, so a worker adds on top. Decorations lift farm income like any shop's.
- Petani works kebun and sawah (1 and 2 slots per level). Peternak works kandang and kolam. Pengepul works the Bank Sampah, which had no job before. All three pay a little rupiah and nudge Patience up, so they are the calm, boring jobs next to ojol and buzzer.
- Sawah needs 8 residents, Kolam Lele needs 1★, Kandang Ayam needs a warung. Farms are mid-game buys, not the first purchase.
- Kandang Ayam costs 0.3 Patience a month for the noise, so a farm is not free money.
- Farm plots are flat, bare tiles that draw their own pematang edge. They join the flat sets so the render skips the building shadow.
