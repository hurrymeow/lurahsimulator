# Icons use shapes everyone knows from phones

The old people glyph and Orang Dalam and Virality icons needed a legend. Pixel art stays, but each icon now draws the shape people already know.

- Rupiah: gold coin. Popularitas: red heart. Orang Dalam: key (access). Virality: flame (trending). Population: person head and shoulders.
- The top bar "Pop" text is now the person icon. Its tap card uses the same icon.
- Menu: Build is a mallet glyph and Residents is a person glyph, in place of the tiny scenes. Neighbours, Wacana and Combo keep their sprite scenes.
- Log, stats, how-to, settings and install glyphs already read as list, bars, ?, gear and arrow, so they are unchanged.
