# Settings help moves to the controls

Following the icon-first plan, the eight redundant help lines in Settings move to `title` and `aria-description`, so the detail still shows on long-press or hover without adding words to the first view. Reduce motion keeps its line because its "follows your phone" detail changes behaviour. The `settings` `wordsInView` cap is 35, the highest measured count (31 at 412 and 430px) rounded up to the next 5.
