# The objective, names, Inem and warga pressure (Proposal 1)

Julian's design review, approved by Hari on 2026-10-28. Measured on main before this: the builder bot sat at the Popularitas cap of 100 from year 6 in 2 of 3 seeds, 21 of 54 residents could be jobless with no cost, and one policy gave 4.8 Popularitas in total against a Satpam's 1 a month.

- **Objective.** Inem's opener names it: make your kampung sejahtera before Sidang Emas 2045. "2045" is the name of the final review, not a calendar year: the game counts Year 1, Year 2, and the three stages alone take about 39 game years.
- **Sejahtera on the 2045 meter.** The top bar's meter now shows Sejahtera (the average of Perut, Kerja and Aman) instead of a value that crept up 0.02% a year. `s.emas` still feeds the yearly ranking as before. Tapping it explains the three meters, any hungry households or preman, and the Laporan score.
- **Laporan.** Residents + 5 per announcement + 5 per program carried out + Popularitas / 2 + 10 per ranking win. It is shown, not yet used: the endings come with the top act.
- **Names.** New Game asks for Pak or Bu, a name and a kampung, each with a dice (12 characters at most, flower and fruit kampung names, no real places). `t()` fills `{pt}`, `{pn}` and `{pk}` in any string, and uses a key's `.bu` twin when the player is Bu. Old saves play as "Pak RT" of "Kampung Kenanga".
- **Inem.** A 44x60 portrait drawn as palette rows in `src/render/inem.ts` (so it is code like every sprite), shown at 2x on every message and event card and at 1x in the opener bar. Her opener and the first-build "well done" use the player's names; the "well done" flirts a little with Pak and is a best-friend line for Bu. Her dangdut arc and her place as staff number 1 come with the staff system.
- **Perut, Kerja, Aman, preman.** Numbers in GAME-DESIGN.md section 4. Aman falls with crowding and with idle warga, so preman can actually appear; Pos Satpam now opens with the first preman, and Satpam posts are held for preman while one waits. Preman rolls hash the seed, month and resident, so they never shift the shared random stream.
- **Wacana or Realisasi.** Each policy row in the panel now carries its real program under the announcement.
- **Popularitas.** A soft cap above 80 (gains shrink toward 0 at 100), employed gain 0.08 to 0.04, expectations 0.1 to 0.14 per resident, Satpam 1 to 0.5. On the bot a fully built kampung now settles at 90 to 94 instead of 100, and an early kampung sits in the 60s. The proposal's 55 to 80 band was not reached for a bot that owns every building; it is a lower bound on a real player's spare Popularitas, so the soft cap is the honest fix rather than another round of trimming.
- **Balance bot.** It answers each preman with a Pos Satpam, as a player would. All pacing targets pass.
