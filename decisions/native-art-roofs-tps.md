# Native-size big art, tidy edges, roof shapes, TPS

- **Big buildings are drawn at their own size, not scaled up.** `new Painter(n)` takes the same one-tile coordinates and rasterises at n times. A 2x2 or 3x3 sprite's edges and diagonals are smooth 1px 2:1 lines. Before, a one-tile sprite was scaled up and re-inked, and the ink traced its 2-3px stair steps, which read as scalloped edges. Paint callbacks still get one-tile rows and columns, so windows, stripes and roof courses keep their proportions. One-tile art is pixel-identical: all 132 one-tile sprites were hashed before and after. The only ones that changed are the upgrade-kit levels, on purpose.
- **Borders are measured in pixels.** `ground()` passes `px`, the size of one canvas pixel in a or b. Kerbs, bunds and rims are written as a number of pixels, so they stay one or two pixels wide on a 3x3 field. Before, they were fractions of the lot and grew with it into thick skirts.
- **Lapangan:** a 1px coloured ink edge, a 1px light stone kerb inside it, then sunken chalk lines: a 1px white line with a 1px darker shadow under it. This replaces the thick dark band and wide white skirt.
- **Standalone flat pieces get a dither,** a half-transparent checker of the edge colour on the grass just outside, so they settle into the ground. Tiling pieces (jalan, selokan, sawah) skip it, because it would bleed onto their neighbours.
- **The upgrade kit is native 1px detail,** drawn after the ink pass (`p.overlay`), so it is never outlined into a chunky band. Level 2 is a tidy wooden plank fence. Level 3 is a clean stone wall with a light cap, pots and a lamp.
- **Roof shapes after contemporary Indonesian perumahan:** limasan hip, pelana (gable) either way round, mono-pitch shed, and flat dak beton with a parapet round a recessed deck. Each rumah's uid picks roof colour, shape and wall colour, so a street looks built over the years. Roof rises keep every shape at or under the hipped roof's apex, so no house grew taller.
- **Walls breathe:** a band of roster vents under the eave and weep holes along the base on every house, batu alam on minimalis fronts, a vent in each gable end, and a tandon on flat roofs. The kos-kosan's top level is a modern kost with a flat dak, a roster band and a tandon.
- **Each shop type has its own roof,** so a row of shops reads as a street. Warteg and bengkel are pelana, warkop and barbershop are shed, fotokopi and apotek are flat, koperasi is a turned pelana, and kontrakan has one long shed roof.
- **Bank sampah is a TPS,** a trash collection point: an open concrete bak heaped with bags and karung, a green signboard, and no roof. Level 2 adds the three standard bins (organik green, anorganik yellow, B3 red) and an orange gerobak sampah.
